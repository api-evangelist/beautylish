# Beautylish

> Beautylish is a San Francisco based online beauty retailer and community, founded in 2010 by Nils Johnson, Vu Nguyen and Sameer Iyengar. It started as a community for sharing beauty tutorials, reviews and product knowledge and launched its own e-commerce storefront in 2012. Beautylish curates makeup, skincare, hair, fragrance, nails, bath and body, wellness and tool brands from around the world and pairs them with editorial content and makeup-artist guidance.

## API surface

Beautylish publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-06 there is no developer
portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDKs and no
webhook documentation on any Beautylish host. `developer.beautylish.com` and
`developers.beautylish.com` do not resolve. Every `/.well-known/` discovery path
on `www.beautylish.com` returns 404, including `security.txt`, `api-catalog`,
`openid-configuration`, `ai-plugin.json` and both A2A agent-card locations.

The company's public partner surface is a ShareASale/Awin affiliate program,
not an API. The `github.com/beautylish` organization holds three public
repositories, all forks of third-party projects (PitneyBowes shipping API
client, PyYAML, 3dbinpacking) — no first-party client library.

If you are an agent looking for a programmatic path to Beautylish product,
order or account data: there is not one. Do not infer endpoints from the
storefront.
